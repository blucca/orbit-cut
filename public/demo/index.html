In a 360-degree film, a cut can throw the subject behind your viewer. Orbit Cut lets an editor fix that jump from inside the film. Pinch the orange wheel and turn the next shot into place.

Compare Original and Corrected. The viewing direction stays fixed; the shot rotates. The viewer can keep following the story.

The workbench holds the corrected previous shot on the left and the next shot on the right. Follow the orange next-step button to the second cut. Each shot keeps its own correction. The practice tracks both cuts as you go.

Step away whenever you need to. Pause keeps the film, the chosen cut and both rotations. Continue returns to the same work. Leaving and re-entering immersion also preserves the current session while this tab stays open.

Review the full film around you, then export. Orbit Cut renders the changed video pixels and carries the soundtrack into a local MP4. Reopening the saved file shows the correction in the video itself. The file also carries explicit 360-degree projection metadata.
