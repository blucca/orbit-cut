# An eight-minute Quest editing trial

[Open Orbit Cut](https://blucca.github.io/orbit-cut/) in the Quest browser. Sit at the workbench and use the built-in synthetic film; you can keep your own footage private.

**The job:** keep the round beacon in the same direction through both cuts, then save a corrected film.

Start a timer when you enter the immersive workbench. Stop after eight minutes, even if a step is unfinished.

1. Follow the orange next-step button. Correct the first cut with your hands, then select **Go to Cut 2** and correct it. Watch the alignment progress.
2. Select **Pause**, then **Continue**. Check that the second cut and both corrections are still there.
3. Open **Review in 360**. Compare **Original** with **Corrected** and look around. Use the playback pause to compare one frame.
4. Export the corrected MP4. Leave immersion to save it. Select **Continue editing**, open the saved file in Orbit Cut, and check the result again. Its pixels already contain your changes.

Please reply to the person who invited you, or [open a GitHub issue](https://github.com/blucca/orbit-cut/issues/new), with:

- Your Quest model and browser; whether you normally edit 360 video.
- The last step completed and elapsed time; any hint you needed.
- The first confusing or awkward moment, and whether the result kept the subject in view.
- Whether pausing retained your place and edits.
- Whether saving and reopening worked; the export format shown by the app, if available.

A short screen recording is useful if you choose to share one. Written observations are equally welcome. Thank you for trying a real editing task.
